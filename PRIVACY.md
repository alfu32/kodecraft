# Privacy policy

Kode (published as **kodecraft** in the Microsoft Store and **kode-rs** in the Snap Store) doesn't collect, sell or share your personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and the developers receive nothing from your use of the app.

## What stays on your computer

Kode saves these on your own computer so it can pick up where you left off:

- Open files, cursor positions, undo history and unsaved changes.
- Its code index, in your project folder.
- Database connections and REST collections you create. Passwords are stored separately from connection definitions.

Deleting these files removes the data.

## When Kode uses the network

Kode only connects to the network when you ask it to:

- **REST client:** sends your requests to the addresses you enter, including any credentials you add.
- **Databases:** connects to the servers you configure, and downloads drivers you choose to install.
- **Language servers and Java:** downloads the optional components you choose to install.
- **OAuth sign-in:** opens a temporary local address to receive the sign-in response from the provider you choose.
- **Browser mode:** `kode serve` makes the app available in a browser, on your own machine unless you choose otherwise.

Those services have their own privacy policies.
